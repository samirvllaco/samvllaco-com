# One theme, type and spacing, layout check, feel consistency, buttons

Moved here from CLAUDE.md on 28 Nov 2026, word for word. These rules load by themselves when a matching file is opened.

## One theme (owner, 22 Nov 2026; replaces "Both themes")
The light/dark switch is gone. The site is one look: dark, with white bands (owner, 27 Nov 2026, like grafit.agency): a section with `band-light` paints white edge to edge and swaps the tokens inside it (home: How I work and FAQ); the band is dark (`.is-dim`, set by `SectionColors.jsx`) until it reaches the 60% line of the screen, then its paint and its color tokens (registered with `@property`) animate to white over .9s and it stays white once scrolled past (owner, 28 Nov 2026: "the top section should stay white, not go black"); it turns dark again only when you scroll back above it (owner, 27 Nov 2026: "not fan of fade sections, should smoothly animate on scroll to desired color"). Use tokens inside bands so they follow; hard-coded light colors need an `.is-dim` dark value. Without JS bands stay white, including a light `--socket` under the 3D buttons. While a lit band covers the bottom of the screen, `html.on-light` (SectionColors) hides the bottom blur and turns the floating Ask pill, book bubble and back-to-top light. Hard-coded dark card colours inside a band need a light override in the "White bands" block of `app/globals.css`. Bent edge (owner, 28 Nov 2026: "using gsap, bend the section, then straight while scrolling away"): the band paint is `.band-light::after`, edge to edge, with a half-ellipse foot `--bend` deep over the next section; SectionColors sets `--bend` from the scroll (full depth when the band's foot enters, flat by the 40% line) and the vendored GSAP eases it.
- A light panel is a section with the `lite` class (tokens swapped at the end of `app/globals.css`). Use tokens inside it; do not use `hero2-btn-light` inside a `lite` panel.
- Old `:root[data-theme="light"]` rules are dead code; do not add new ones.
- Before calling a change done: `npm run build`, `npx serve out -l 4173`, then
  `NODE_PATH=$(npm root -g) node scripts/contrast.cjs` (all pages) or `... scripts/contrast.cjs /page/ /other/`. It must end with 0 failures.

## Type and spacing system (owner, 15 Nov 2026, locked)
Tokens and role rules are the last block of `app/globals.css` ("Type and spacing system"). Use them; never set a one-off heading size.
- Spacing: 4px base, only 4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24, 32, 40, 48, 64, 80, 96, 128. Sections use `--space-section` (56px on phones, 96px at 1440; owner, 27 Nov 2026: "more compact", was 64 to 128), gutters `--gutter` (16 to 40px). Intro blocks (eyebrow, title, lede) 12px apart; card grids 20px.
- Widths: content 1280px (`--max`; was 1200 until 26 Nov 2026), prose 760px (`--prose`), hero headline 920px.
- Type roles: display h1 56/60, section title h2 40/48, panel headline 28/36, all three at weight 500 (owner, 27 Nov 2026: "thinner titles and more compact"; were 64/68 and 48/56 at 600), subhead 24/32, card title 20/28, lede 18/28. Negative tracking everywhere (owner, 27 Nov 2026: "the standard now"): -4% on display, -3.5% on section titles, -2% on card and panel titles, -1.5% on ledes, -1.1% on body text; only uppercase eyebrows and handwritten notes keep their own. Nothing on the site heavier than 600.
- One ink, one accent; secondary text is the ink at lower opacity or `--muted`.

## Layout check (owner, 26 Nov 2026: "do full layout check, make sure we follow it strictly")
Inside every section: 12px between eyebrow, title and lede (`--gap-intro`); 40px from the intro to the content, between content groups and from the content to its button (`--gap-content`, 32px on phones; 48 until 27 Nov 2026); 20px between cards and between a card grid and its chips or filters (`--gap-grid`); card padding on the spacing scale (24 for cards, 32 for big panels). Sections sit `--space-section` apart; where sections have padding and a hairline divider, the line sits halfway. Every panel, grid and card row spans the 1280px content box or starts at its left edge; only text, product shots and mockups are centered narrower. The rules live in the last blocks of `app/globals.css` ("Layout system, strict").
Before calling a layout change done, with `npx serve out -l 4173` running: `NODE_PATH=$(npm root -g) node scripts/layout.cjs` (all pages) or `... scripts/layout.cjs /page/`. It must end with 0 layout problems.

Three stat cells, each a real number with a picture of it: years on a gauge (2013 to today), 573 dots for 573 clients, the 48h turnaround on a timeline; the work deck is one small cell where the screens stat was (owner, 28 Nov 2026: "instead of 10k screens designed put this on that place and remove 10k screens"). The toolbox runs the full width under them, words left and the tools drifting sideways on the right (`ToolTrack`; owner, 28 Nov 2026: "make them scroll like before", the tilted tiles are gone); "See all" opens the 6-column tool grid. Stat cells (owner, 28 Nov 2026: "improve animation here + alignment + use GSAP to add effects, depth and make it feel super smooth"): every stat cell has the same three rows, the label, a 152px picture box with the picture centred and the number at the foot, so pictures and numbers line up; a soft blue light follows the pointer in the cell. With GSAP (`BentoRun.jsx`, `.gs`): the gauge needle sweeps from 2013 to today and lights each tick as it passes, with a glow and a halo under the arc; the client dots light in a wave with a bright front and a faint shimmer now and then; the turnaround line runs a lit head to "back to you", where a ring opens. Experience is a year slider (owner, 28 Nov 2026: "make this interactive, show years like in About, add light effects, indicate experience, how design changed, styles, tools"): move over the gauge, drag it or use the arrow keys, Home and End; the needle snaps year by year from 2013 to today, the tick under it glows white, the halo follows it and the line under the gauge gives the year and what changed (`lib/years.js`: Sam's own milestones and dated design shifts only). Clients (owner, same day: "make the first dots super big then go smaller to give a feeling of more clients, count and unique; next can be you, a dot to book a call"): the grid starts zoomed in on the first clients as big dots and zooms out to all 573 with the count, every dot has its own shade, and dot 574 is a pulsing "you" link to /book/ ("Client 574 could be you. Book a call"). The grid runs out to the structure rails on wide screens, so its outer edges are the rails (one line, not two). The Worldwide list is gone (owner, 28 Nov 2026: "remove this"), and so is the FactRow above the bento. Numbers count up once in view (`CountUp.jsx`); loops wait for `is-live` and pause off screen.
- Radius (owner, 27 Nov 2026: "reduce round corners everywhere, no round buttons", then "less round all", then "make less round generally, all things, be consistent everywhere"): `--r-card` 8 for cards and panels, `--r-inner` 6 for boxes inside cards, icon tiles and media, `--r-sm` 4 for small parts, chips, pills, faces, round buttons and every button, `--r-bento` 10. No 999px or 50% radius on anything 22px or larger, except spinners and the globe; tiny dots stay round. Never a one-off radius. Highlights (`==text==` in FAQs) are a flat gray box over the whole word at `--r-sm`, no half-height marker.
- Surfaces: one page color #0e0e10 (owner, 28 Nov 2026: "a bit darker, we use a bit too greyish"; was #171719, and before that #000/#0b0b0c/#111112), cards lift above it in three tones (#141416, #1a1a1d, #232326) and one glass for floating bars. The header bar is glass: 70% dark with a 28px blur, a light rim and sheen, quieter links #d1d1d6; over a lit white band it is solid black (owner, 28 Nov 2026: "can be fully black on a white background"; `html.head-on-light`, set by SectionColors). The footer photo stage keeps its near-black (#07070b) because the photo is shot on it. Lines: 8% white for card edges, 12% for dividers, 20% strong.
- Motion: `--ease-out` for everything, `--spring` (one bounce) for pops and presses, durations .15 / .25 / .35 / .5s. Ambient loops stay linear. The scroll reveal keeps its own timing (owner: "keep our scroll animation").
- Smooth scroll: Locomotive Scroll (Lenis inside) on mouse and trackpad only, lerp .2 so the glide is almost unnoticeable (owner, 27 Nov 2026: removed on 26 Nov, back the next day "super subtle"). Keep per-frame paint cheap: no stacks of full-width backdrop blurs on fixed elements (the bottom edge blur is one layer).
- Structure lines (owner, 26 Nov 2026: "use strokes all the way until the end to show structure"): two 12% rails 24px outside the content box run from the top of the page to the end of the footer (1024px and up), and a divider crosses halfway between every two sections, the footer top and the line under the site map included. Dividers run the full width of the screen, edge to edge (owner, 27 Nov 2026). Last block of `app/globals.css` ("Structure lines"). A new section gets its divider automatically when it follows another section; a painted panel or band (`lite`, `book-band`, closing panels) does not.

## Buttons (owner, 13 Nov 2026, locked)
- Only the two button classes in DESIGN.md (Buttons), with the hover and state rules defined there. Hover (owner, 27 Nov 2026, after grafit.agency): the button does not rise; a darker layer wipes in from the left and the icon nudges 3px to the right, never fading or bouncing (owner, 27 Nov 2026: "subtle left to right"). The same wipe runs on the Ask pill (darker, clipped to the pill), article tools and back to top. Nothing rises on hover anywhere and icon tiles never rotate, tilt or grow (last block of `app/globals.css`, "Button hover wipe"). Check every new or moved button on hover before calling a change done.
- Buttons follow the alignment of the text above them: centered under centered text (owner, 25 Nov 2026; DESIGN.md Buttons). Run `NODE_PATH=$(npm root -g) node scripts/btn-align.cjs` after touching buttons or layout; it must list no rows.
- Buttons side by side are the same width (owner, 14 Nov 2026). Put them alone in one row container: the global rule in `globals.css` sizes every button in a row of only `hero2-btn` buttons to the widest one, and stacks them full width on phones.
