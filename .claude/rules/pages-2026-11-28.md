# Owner decisions of 28 Nov 2026: footers, menus, articles, case studies, Slop Score page, white bands

## Footers and menus (owner, 28 Nov 2026)
- Two footers only (owner: "be consistent, have 2 types of footer, long and short; all shorts should look the same, balanced left and right, easy on the eyes"). Long: the screen wall with the closing call, the top line (Sam left, availability and the call right), the site map with Ask AI, the bottom line. Short: only the top line and the bottom line. Short pages are `WALL_OFF` in `components/Footer.jsx` (tools, Slop Score, contact, free audit, work, the ebook). The contact page has the short footer again; its call reads "See my work".
- Resources menu: the list (ebook, digital kit, free audit) without the UI/UX review and the free prompts (owner: "remove these 2, don't make sense"), then the free tools as three screen thumbnails with "All 3 tools".
- The ebook hero's book is the 3D book (`Book3D` with `still eager`): no slide-in above the fold, it turns toward the pointer.
- Ask Sam's AI (owner, 28 Nov 2026): the pill stays in the middle; only on a single case study or article it is a small key at the bottom left. Everywhere it reads Sam's face and "AI" at rest (owner, 28 Nov 2026: "shorten it like on other pages, on hover show the full text, + animation more"): on hover, focus and one peek per visit (`is-peek`, a moment after the first interaction) the key widens with a spring, "Ask Sam's" slides out of a clip and a light sweeps across the chrome. No spark dot on the face anywhere ("don't add a black dot"). Answers type out at an even pace (`Typed` in `AskSam.jsx`) and the list follows the end of the answer unless the visitor scrolls up; the thinking bubble is compact, three dots in a soft wave beside the status words.
- The booking QR (`BookAlt` in `BookBand.jsx`) opens with GSAP: the small code flies to the middle while it grows and the card opens round it; closing flies it back.
- Single articles (owner, 28 Nov 2026): short footer, no wall and no booking band, no call-to-action section (it ends with "More articles", three cards from the same category first, then the related pages), no hire card in the side column, no magnetic dots. The byline has Sam's photo, name and role, then the date and reading time with icons. Copy page and Share keep their width when the label changes.
- On this page (`CaseAside.jsx`, articles and case studies): a 4px rail that GSAP fills with the reading, numbers in their own column, read items brighter, the current one white with a blue number.
- The breadcrumb "Jump to" menu is built from `MAIN_NAV` in `lib/nav.js` (the header's nav items) plus Home and the call; the header's plain links come from the same list. A top nav link stays lit on the pages under it (an article lights Insights).
- The square pattern on call-to-action panels is gone (owner: "don't use it anymore").
- How I work (owner, 28 Nov 2026; `HowIWorkMotion.jsx`, wide and tall screens): as the rail reaches a step one grey box glides behind the whole row, number, title and line (GSAP; owner: "highlight the whole thing, the row, not only the title"; it sweeps in from the left the first time), the title and line turn to full ink and ease 4px in; steps not reached stay quieter. When the last step lights, a spinning colour ring draws round Start a project. The pinned screen sits centred on the steps and the button (`--hw-top`).
- Reading line on case studies and articles (`ReadLine.jsx`): 4px, blue, eased by GSAP.
- Contact: after a message is sent, the hidden calendar no longer holds the height.
- FAQ email pill: the mail icon leads, Copy at the end.
- Work cards (home carousel and /work/): hovering one turns the other screens grey and dim and their titles muted (owner, 28 Nov 2026: "when you highlight one, de-emphasise the others"; this replaces "nothing dims on hover" for work cards only). Words stay at 4.5:1.
- About year (owner, 28 Nov 2026): follows the story part being read (How it started shows 2013 in Prizren, the rest today with their fact) and is a slider: drag it sideways or use the arrow keys, Home and End.
- Ebook hero: only the 3D book (owner: "keep only the book").
- Slop Score page (owner, 28 Nov 2026: "only this, nothing else, simple and user focused; steps with GSAP, next by next"): only the tool under the title. Step 1 the site and Next, step 2 the email (optional, with the follow-up box) and "What you get" (free: score, 7 check scores, top 3 fixes; the paid full report at `SLOP_REPORT_PRICE`) with the 7 checks, then Check my site, step 3 the live scan. GSAP slides between steps and eases the height. A scan that comes with an email saves the lead and starts the report email (`saveLead` in `slopscore/src/api.js`, no second captcha); the report page then says it was sent.
- Email pills: the Copy chip has the same inset on the right as the icon on the left.
- White bands (owner, 28 Nov 2026: "each page should have at least one white section, unless it's a result page, a single page or where we can't"): every FAQ is a white band (`Faq` defaults to `light`), About's "Sound familiar?" (`PainStory light`), every second group on /services/ and "Latest stories" on /resources/. Left dark on purpose: single case studies and articles, Slop Score results, the contact page, the Slop Score and free audit tools, each free tool, and the /work/ and /writing/ lists (one grid each, no section to turn white).
- About portrait on load (owner, 28 Nov 2026, after launch.joinautopilot.com; `PortraitGlow.jsx`): once the photo is in view and the intro is gone, it glitches for under half a second (bands shift sideways with cyan and orange edges), then a stepped, pixelated ring of light with colour fringes opens round it and fades (about 2s). Reduced motion skips it. The portrait layers start from nothing at load, wherever the page opens.
- The FAQ has no magnetic dots (owner: "remove the animated dots on this section"); the FAQ card's LinkedIn mark has fixed colours so it shows with the band, no lag.
- The floating ebook bubble (`BookPopup.jsx`, the book tab bottom left) is off on every page (owner, 28 Nov 2026: "hide completely from all pages"); the component stays unused. The Ask AI key on articles and case studies sits at the bottom left on its own.
- How I work under GSAP (owner, 28 Nov 2026: "using GSAP scroll, animate, snap, smoother, and effects on the right"): on wide, tall screens `HowIWorkMotion.jsx` adds `.hw-gs` and the CSS scroll timeline stands down. The rail eases to the foot of the step you are on (`--railp`; `--rail` is the rail's colour), numbers turn blue as reached, the screen on the right wipes up over the last one (down when going back) while it settles from a slight zoom and its caption rises, and when scrolling stops inside the section the page glides to the nearest step (through `window.__smooth`, the Locomotive instance, or native smooth scroll).
- Start and end of single articles and case studies (owner, 28 Nov 2026: "article should have a good start and a good end, we should know when the article ends", "same for single work"): the article header closes with a hairline under the byline row; `EndMark.jsx` ends the content with a hairline marked "End of article" or "End of case study" and a sign-off card (Sam's face, who wrote it and when, Back to top). On case studies it sits before the closing call. No call to action inside it.
- On this page (articles and case studies, 1100px and up) sits on its own lighter panel (#141416, 8% rim) so the side column reads apart from the page (owner, 28 Nov 2026).
- Free tools have no booking band: /tools/, each tool and the Slop Score (owner, 28 Nov 2026: "hide this from free tools").
- Slop Score steps are centred under the centred title, and the step box has padding inside its clip so focus rings and button glows are never cut (owner, 28 Nov 2026: "middle align and don't crop on left").
- Slop Score loading (owner, 28 Nov 2026: "make sure it always loads"): `open()` in `slopscore/src/scan.js` acts like desktop Chrome, tries the address as typed, then with or without www, then http (`variants` in `url.js`, tested); a slow page that already drew is scanned anyway; an error status in front of a real page is scanned; only a challenge or empty error page is "bot_blocked". The browser start and the scanner call are retried once. Failures keep a short detail in D1 `scan_errors`.
- Lines never cross content (owner, 28 Nov 2026: "make sure lines don't overlap"): the structure rails sit under the page content (body is its own stacking context, rails at z-index -1), so cards that run edge to edge cover them; the review rows fade at their foot with a page-coloured veil, not a mask, so no line shows through a faded card.
- Case study screens (owner, 28 Nov 2026: "use GSAP animation on single work as well to reveal work and show them in creative layouts"): the screens run in an editorial rhythm on wide screens (`shotCls` in app/work/[slug]/page.jsx: one wide, a pair with the narrow one set 96px lower, a pair the other way round; a pair left with one screen gives it the full width). `CaseMotion.jsx` opens each screen from a soft inset clip and a slight zoom as it scrolls in, lifts its caption a few pixels (transform only) and lets the lower screen of each pair drift slower than the page. Screens on the first screen never start hidden; reduced motion shows everything still.
- A live product's case study ends with "Check live" (the work item's `live` address, shown in the end card too): the eye care marketing site links to ocufuse.com (owner, 28 Nov 2026).
